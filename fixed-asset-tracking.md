# Fixed Asset Tracking - Computer Hardware

## Context
Accounting is cleaning up the fixed asset list of computer hardware. They will send a list by location for IT to review, mark disposals, and provide approximate disposal dates.

## Required Information Per Device (From Accounting Memo)

What accounting needs from IT:
- **Asset Tag / Asset Number** — unique identifier tying the physical device to the financial record
- **Device Description** — type of hardware (laptop, desktop, monitor, docking station, etc.)
- **Location** — office/site where the device is assigned
- **Current Status** — in use, in storage, or disposed
- **Disposition Status** — whether the device has been disposed of (no longer in possession)
- **Approximate Disposal Date** — when the device left company possession (if disposed)

## Industry Standard Fields for Fixed Asset Registers (GAAP / IRS)

Beyond what accounting explicitly asked for, a complete fixed asset record typically includes:

- **Asset Tag / ID** — unique company-assigned identifier
- **Serial Number** — manufacturer serial for positive identification
- **Device Make & Model** — e.g., Dell Latitude 5540, HP EliteDesk 800 G9
- **Device Type / Category** — laptop, desktop, monitor, peripheral, networking, etc.
- **Purchase Date** — date the asset was acquired
- **Purchase Cost** — original acquisition cost (including tax/shipping if capitalized)
- **Vendor / Supplier** — where it was purchased from
- **PO Number / Invoice Number** — links back to the purchase transaction
- **Location / Site** — physical location of the asset
- **Assigned User** — employee the device is assigned to
- **Department** — department charged for the asset
- **Useful Life** — expected service life (IRS default for computers: 5 years)
- **Depreciation Method** — straight-line, MACRS, Section 179, bonus depreciation
- **Accumulated Depreciation** — total depreciation taken to date
- **Net Book Value (NBV)** — cost minus accumulated depreciation
- **Warranty Expiration** — end of manufacturer/extended warranty
- **Condition** — new, good, fair, end-of-life
- **Status** — active, spare/storage, in repair, disposed, lost/stolen
- **Disposal Date** — date removed from service / company possession
- **Disposal Method** — sold, recycled, donated, scrapped, returned (lease)
- **Disposal Value / Salvage Value** — amount recovered at disposal (if any)
- **Certificate of Destruction / Recycling** — proof of proper disposal (data security + environmental compliance)

## IRS / GAAP Notes

- **Capitalization threshold**: assets above a set dollar amount (commonly $500-$2,500 under de minimis safe harbor) are capitalized rather than expensed
- **MACRS 5-year property**: computers and peripherals fall under 5-year MACRS for tax depreciation
- **Section 179 / Bonus Depreciation**: allows full or accelerated write-off in the year of purchase
- **Disposal requires gain/loss calculation**: proceeds minus NBV at disposal date
